I have joined AMD as a Senior Member of Technical Staff (Senior MTS), working on AI for hardware design.

Before joining AMD, I was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Florida State University and a Research Scientist at Samsung Research America. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh in 2024, advised by Prof. Heng Huang. I earned my M.S. in Computer Systems Engineering from Northeastern University in 2017 and my B.S. in Electronic Engineering from Xidian University in 2015.
